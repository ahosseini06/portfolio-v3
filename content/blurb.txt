My family moved to Canada when I was nine. I didn’t understand finances, but I understood that we needed money. Nine-year-old me took the problem into his own hands.

I knew a few magic tricks. I took my act to local playgrounds, where I’d perform for parents and offer to do their kids’ birthday parties.

That nine-year-old problem solver is the entrepreneur in me today. I usually have at least three projects going, all to solve problems way bigger than myself.

The one I’m betting on for the foreseeable future is Nootles. I want to give people back the power to create and collaborate while keeping the speed AI gives them. Work should be fun again.
